Private by design.

Front Row runs in Apple's App Sandbox. It sees only what you hand it, and nothing about you leaves your Mac.

🔒

Only the folders you pick

Your library is read-only, and only the folders you add. Nothing else on your Mac is reachable.

👤

Sign-ins stay put

Each profile keeps its own logins, inside the app. Front Row has no account and no servers.

📡

No tracking

No analytics, no ads, no data collected. It only looks up film details and artwork.
